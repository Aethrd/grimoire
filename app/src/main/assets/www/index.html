      From the web

      Chapters from a website, for Build

      Paste the address of a book's contents page. Every link on it is listed — the chapters, and whatever else the site put there. Untick what is not the book, and the rest goes into Build as one part, a chapter a page, downloaded when you build. More than one address, one per line, and their links are put together — the same book on other sites, after the first, fills in the chapters the first is missing, and stands in for any of its chapters that will not come.
